Core Services

Interior & Exterior Finishing

Smashview Global Resources Limited provides comprehensive finishing services for residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Our interior and exterior finishing division covers the full lifecycle of a space — from concept design through material selection to on-site installation and final handover.

Interior Finishing

  • Space planning, room layout design, and interior concept development
    • Residential apartments, offices, hotels, retail outlets, and hospitality spaces
  • Wall finishing: plastering, screeding, skim-coating, dry-lining, and decorative renders
  • Floor laying: tiles, marble, granite, hardwood, laminate, vinyl, and epoxy flooring
  • Ceiling installation: suspended ceilings, coffered ceilings, POP, gypsum board, and acoustic panels
    • Baffle, linear, and metallic strip systems for commercial interiors
  • Joinery and millwork: built-in wardrobes, kitchen cabinetry, bespoke furniture, and timber panelling
  • Painting and décor finishes: textured coatings, feature walls, and specialist paint systems
  • Lighting design and installation: ambient, task, accent, and architectural lighting
  • Curtain walling, blinds, drapes, and soft furnishing supply and installation
  • Bathroom and kitchen fit-out: sanitary ware, plumbing fixtures, and countertops

Exterior Finishing

  • Façade design and cladding: aluminium composite panels, glass curtain walls, stone veneer, and rendered finishes
  • External plastering, textured render, and weatherproof coating systems
  • Roofing: flat roof membranes, pitched roofing, skylights, and roof insulation
  • External tiling and paving: driveways, courtyards, walkways, and pool surrounds
  • Landscaping and hardscaping: boundary walls, fencing, gates, and garden structures
  • Exterior painting and anti-corrosion coating for steel and concrete surfaces
  • Signage, branding installations, and external lighting

General Contracting

As a general contractor, Smashview Global Resources Limited delivers end-to-end construction management across residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional project types. We coordinate all trades, subcontractors, and suppliers under a single contract — giving clients one point of accountability from ground-breaking to handover.

Contracting Activities

  • New construction: residential buildings, commercial complexes, warehouses, and industrial facilities
  • Structural works: foundations, reinforced concrete frames, steel structures, and masonry
  • Renovation and refurbishment of existing structures, including heritage and distressed buildings
  • Project management and site supervision — scheduling, budgeting, and quality control
  • Procurement of labour, plant, equipment, and specialist subcontractors
  • MEP coordination: mechanical, electrical, and plumbing works integration
  • Civil works: drainage, site clearance, road formation, and earthworks
  • Container-based modular construction: design, fabrication, and installation
  • Fit-out contracting for offices, retail, hospitality, and healthcare environments
  • Post-construction snagging, commissioning, and defects liability management
  • Compliance with Nigerian Building Code and relevant local authority requirements

Import & Export of Building Materials

Smashview Global Resources Limited operates an active import and export division, sourcing premium building materials, finishes, and specialist products from international markets and distributing them nationwide across Nigeria. Our trade division bridges the gap between global supply and local demand — ensuring clients have access to quality materials at competitive terms.

Import Activities

  • Premium tiles, marble, granite, and engineered stone from Italy, Spain, China, and Turkey
  • Aluminium profiles, curtain wall systems, and composite cladding panels
  • Suspended ceiling systems: aluminium baffle, linear strip, and acoustic tile panels
  • Sanitary ware, bathroom fittings, kitchen fixtures, and plumbing hardware
  • Electrical fittings, lighting systems, and smart home components
  • Paints, coatings, adhesives, sealants, and specialist finishing products
  • Steel, structural sections, roofing sheets, and prefabricated components
  • Doors, windows, curtain wall glazing, and architectural hardware
  • Full customs clearance, freight forwarding, and last-mile delivery coordination

Export Activities

  • Export of locally available building materials and natural resources to international buyers
  • Documentation, export licences, and shipping coordination
  • Quality inspection and certification for exported goods
  • Liaison with Nigerian Export Promotion Council (NEPC) and relevant agencies

Nationwide Distribution

  • Delivery to project sites and trade buyers across all 36 states and FCT
  • Warehousing, inventory management, and bulk supply to contractors and developers
  • Just-in-time delivery scheduling aligned to project construction programmes
